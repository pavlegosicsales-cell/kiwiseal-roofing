What we do

Repair the faults first,
then seal the lot

Paint hides a bad roof. A membrane only works if what sits under it is sound. So every job starts with the mechanical work: rust treatment, rescrewing sheets, replacing broken tiles, repointing ridge caps. The coating goes on after that, not instead of it.

Four branches cover Auckland and Northland, Waikato, Bay of Plenty and Wellington. Our own trained crews do the work, so the person who quotes the job is accountable for it.

How it works

Three steps, and most roofs take two to four days

Step 01

Free roof assessment

We come out, get on the roof and write down what is actually wrong with it. You get a fixed, all inclusive price covering repairs, scaffolding and the membrane.

Step 02

Repairs, clean and prep

Chemical clean to kill the moss, then a water blast. We patch, rescrew, replace broken tiles and treat the rust before a drop of membrane goes on.

Step 03

Membrane and handover

Two coats of self priming membrane, a final inspection, then the site gets cleaned up. Your 10 year warranty follows in writing.
