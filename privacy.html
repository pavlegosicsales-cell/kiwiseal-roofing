Privacy policy

Short version: we collect what you type into the enquiry form, we use it to quote your roof, and you can ask us to delete it whenever you like.

Last updated: 11 September 2026.

This policy explains how Kiwiseal Roofing handles the personal information you send us through this website. We handle it in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and the information privacy principles it sets out.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form or the call back field:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • The property address or suburb
  • The service, roof type, roof condition, region and timing you selected
  • Any notes you choose to write in the message field

We do not ask for and do not collect identity document numbers, payment card details, or any of the sensitive categories of information the Act treats differently.

What we use it for

We use your details to answer your enquiry, book a roof assessment and put a quote together. If you go ahead with the work, we use the same details to keep in touch while the job runs and to issue your warranty afterwards.

We do not send marketing to people who have not asked for it.

Who sees it, and for how long

Enquiries land in our business inbox and are passed to the branch covering your region. We do not sell your details and we do not pass them to third parties for marketing.

We may share what is needed with people working on your job, such as a repair partner, a scaffolding supplier or a materials supplier, and only to the extent the job requires it.

We keep your information for as long as we need it to deal with you and to meet our legal and warranty obligations, then we delete it.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set tracking cookies and does not run advertising networks. If we add a visitor measurement tool later, we will update this page before we turn it on.

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser sends its IP address to that service when a page loads.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email sales@kiwisealroofing.co.nz or call 0800 123 587 and we will come back to you within 20 working days, as the Act requires.

If you think we have handled your information poorly and we have not put it right, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
